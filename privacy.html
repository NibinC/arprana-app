Privacy Policy

AR-PRANA: Energized Healing · Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains what the AR-PRANA app (“the app”, “we”) does with your information. In short: your meditation practice stays on your device, we don't track you, and we don't sell data.

Information that stays on your device

Meditation history, Peaceful Impact (calm) scores, program progress and reflections, sound-bath settings and your jewelry order history are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to us or shared with anyone, and they work without an internet connection.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on, the app writes completed sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. The app does not read any Health data. Health information is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties.

Motion data (optional)

With supported AirPods, head-movement data is used on device, in real time, to keep spatial audio anchored around you. It is not stored or transmitted.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive only confirmation of whether your subscription is active; we never see your payment details.

Session requests with Ardra

When you request a live session, the request is composed in your own email app and sent to us only when you press Send. We use the name, email address and details you include solely to arrange your session.

Energized jewelry orders

When jewelry ordering is available and you place an order, Apple Pay shares the shipping name, address, email address and phone number you approve. We use them only to make, ship and support your order. Card payments are processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy; the app never sees your card number. Your personal intention note and recipient name are used only to prepare your piece and certificate.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

You can delete everything stored by the app at any time in Settings › Delete Account / Clear Data. Deleting the app also removes its data. For order or session records we hold, email us and we'll delete them unless we must keep them by law (for example, tax records).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above.
